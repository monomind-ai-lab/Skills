# EGM distribution boundary

`upstream/` is an unmodified third-party snapshot, not this catalog's operating
policy. Do not edit its files individually. Refresh only through an explicitly
authorized whole-snapshot curation task, preserving licenses and verifying the
inventory in `upstreams/egm.json` at the catalog root.

Upstream contribution instructions govern contributions to EGM itself; they do
not replace Monomind repository policy or a consuming project's instructions.
Bundled hooks and CI files are source artifacts, not permission to enable them.
